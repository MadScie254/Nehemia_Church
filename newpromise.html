A Home for Every Child

New Promise Home

"Religion that God our Father accepts as pure and faultless is this: to look after orphans and widows in their distress." — James 1:27

How New Promise Home Was Born

From a burden in prayer to a refuge of love.

New Promise Home did not begin with a building. It began with a broken heart. Bishop David Walukhu and the community at Nehemia Worship Center could not look away from the faces of children in Mukhonje who had no family, no shelter, and no one to call their own. The Bible was clear: true religion means caring for the orphan and the widow. The question was not whether to act — it was how fast they could build what love demanded.

What began as informal care — meals, school fees, clothing — grew into a structured home where children are not just sheltered but raised. New Promise Home gives every child a name, a bed, an education, healthcare, and most importantly, a family that knows and calls them by name. Here, every child learns that they are not accidents of circumstance — they are children of a God who never forgets, never abandons, and always provides.

Today, New Promise Home stands as a living testimony that when the church takes the Gospel seriously, it does not just preach deliverance — it builds the places where deliverance lives.

What We Provide

Every child deserves more than survival — they deserve to flourish.

Safe Shelter

A clean, stable, and loving home environment where children feel protected and cherished every day.

Education

Full school enrollment, uniforms, tutoring support, and preparation for a future of purpose and opportunity.

Healthcare

Regular medical checkups, nutrition planning, and access to treatment for every child in our care.

Spiritual Formation

Bible studies, prayer, worship, and mentorship to help every child discover their identity in Christ.

Family Reunification

Where possible, we work toward safe family reconnection, providing counseling and transition support.

Identity & Belonging

Every child is called by name, celebrated on birthdays, and raised to know they are loved and valued.
