Ministries / Huduma Zetu

Many members, one body, one mission in Christ.

Children's Ministry — Watoto wa Mungu

Our Children's Ministry is a joyful environment where little hearts learn big truths about Jesus. Through age-appropriate teaching, songs, and activities, watoto are discipled in love and biblical identity.

We partner with parents to build godly foundations that shape character, faith, and prayer habits from an early age. Every child is seen, valued, and mentored with care.

Youth Ministry — Vijana wa Kristo

Vijana wa Kristo exists to raise a generation rooted in truth and bold in purpose. We disciple young people through mentorship, worship, Bible study, and practical life guidance.

Our heartbeat is to help vijana overcome pressure, discover calling, and become spiritual influencers in school, campus, and society.

Women's Ministry — Wanawake wa Nguvu

Wanawake wa Nguvu is a sisterhood of prayer, wisdom, and spiritual growth. Women are equipped to flourish in faith, family, ministry, and purpose through regular fellowship and teaching.

This ministry provides encouragement, counseling support, and leadership formation for women of all ages.

Meeting Time: Saturday 2:00 PM (Placeholder)

Men's Fellowship — Wanaume wa Imani

Wanaume wa Imani develops men of conviction, integrity, and servant leadership. Through fellowship, accountability, and Scripture, men are strengthened to lead their homes and communities in righteousness.

We challenge one another to become examples of consistency in prayer, responsibility, and spiritual authority.

Worship Ministry — Ibada na Sifa

Ibada na Sifa leads the congregation into authentic adoration and reverent praise. We believe worship prepares hearts for encounter and creates an atmosphere where God heals and speaks.

This team serves with musical excellence, humility, and deep spiritual sensitivity.

Prayer Ministry — Sala na Maombi

Sala na Maombi is the intercessory engine of Nehemia Worship Center. We stand in the gap for families, leaders, communities, and national transformation through fervent and disciplined prayer.

This ministry trains believers in intercession, spiritual warfare, and prophetic prayer aligned with Scripture.

Evangelism & Outreach — Injili kwa Dunia

Injili kwa Dunia carries the Gospel beyond church walls into markets, homes, schools, and surrounding communities. We are committed to compassionate mission that meets spiritual and practical needs.

From open-air evangelism to community support initiatives, this ministry mobilizes believers to be witnesses of Christ's love in word and action.
