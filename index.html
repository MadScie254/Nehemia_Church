Who We Are

A House of Restoration in Lugari

Nehemia Ministry Kenya was birthed in prayer, formed in sacrifice, and sustained by the unfailing mercy of God. In Mukhonje Market and beyond, we have witnessed broken lives rebuilt through worship, discipleship, and pastoral care.

Our founding spirit is simple and powerful: when God calls us to rebuild, no ruin is too deep and no heart is too far gone. We gather as one family to lift Christ high and equip believers for daily victory.

Our mission is to raise mature disciples, strengthen homes, and carry the Gospel with bold compassion into every generation.

"The God of heaven will give us success; we his servants will start rebuilding." — Nehemiah 2:20
