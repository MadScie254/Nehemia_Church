About Nehemia Ministry Kenya

Our Story

Nehemiah 2:20 — "The God of heaven will give us success; we his servants will start rebuilding."

Our Story

A journey of faith, obedience, and restoration in Kakamega County.

Nehemia Ministry Kenya was born from a burden in prayer: to see lives rebuilt by the power of the Gospel and communities restored by the love of Christ. In Mukhonje Market, what began as humble fellowship gatherings became a living testimony of God's faithfulness. Families found healing, young people found identity, and weary hearts found new strength in the presence of the Lord.

Through seasons of growth and challenge, the ministry remained anchored in one conviction: when God calls His people to rebuild, He also provides grace, wisdom, and strength to finish the work. Under faithful pastoral covering, the church has grown into a spiritual home where worship is deep, teaching is sound, and prayer is fervent.

We are a church for the broken, the hopeful, the mature believer, and the first-time seeker. At Nehemia Ministry Kenya, no one is beyond God's reach. We believe every person carries purpose, and every family can become a testimony of restoration through Christ Jesus.

Today, our mission continues with renewed fire: to disciple believers, raise kingdom leaders, strengthen homes, and carry the message of hope throughout Lugari Constituency and beyond. We are not merely preserving a legacy; we are building for generations yet to come.

Mission, Vision, Values

The pillars that shape our worship, witness, and walk with Christ.

Mission

To rebuild lives through the transforming power of God's Word, fervent prayer, compassionate discipleship, and Christ-centered fellowship.

Vision

To raise a spiritually mature church family that influences Lugari, Kakamega, Kenya, and the nations with hope and holiness.

Values

Prayer, Scripture, integrity, unity, excellence, servanthood, evangelism, and sacrificial love define the culture of our house.
