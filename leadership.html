Leadership Profile

Bishop David Walukhu

Bishop, Nehemia Worship Center

Bishop David Walukhu is a shepherd with a fathering mantle whose ministry has brought restoration to families, healing to broken hearts, and spiritual awakening to communities. His leadership carries depth in Scripture, steadfastness in prayer, and compassion for every generation.

With decades of faithful ministry, he continues to equip leaders, raise disciples, and call the church to holy living and kingdom impact. His voice remains a pillar of wisdom as Nehemia Worship Center expands its reach across Western Kenya and beyond — with 15 worship centers, a Bible Training College, and New Promise Home orphanage standing as testimony to what God builds when His people obey.

"And I told them of the hand of my God which was good upon me." — Nehemiah 2:18

A Family Built on Faith

The Walukhu Family

Alongside his call to shepherd thousands, Bishop David walks a quieter, equally sacred calling — that of husband and father. He is married to Selina Walukhu, a woman of deep prayer, quiet strength, and unwavering faith whose life is itself a ministry. Together they have built what they preach: a home saturated in Scripture, anchored in worship, and open to God's purposes at every season.

Bishop David and Mama Selina are parents to five children, each one a living testimony that when a family chooses to follow God fully, He fills every room with His presence. Their family is not separate from the ministry — it is the ministry made visible.

"As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord." — Joshua 24:15

First Lady & Partner in Ministry

Selina Walukhu

Pastoral Partner, Nehemia Worship Center

Selina is known for her devotion to prayer, care for families, and quiet leadership that strengthens the fabric of our church. A ministry of hospitality and intercession, she invests deeply in women's discipleship, counseling, and support for the vulnerable.

"She is clothed with strength and dignity; she can laugh at the days to come." — Proverbs 31:25

Her encouragement to our congregation is simple and life-giving: "Hold fast to the Lord, lean into prayer, and let love lead your hands in service. Your faithfulness in small things opens doors for God to do great things through you."

Leadership Profile

Pastor Murunga

Senior Pastor, Nehemia Worship Center

Pastor Murunga serves as a pastor-teacher with a heart for discipleship, community care, and practical Christian living. His ministry style blends biblical clarity with pastoral warmth, guiding believers from spiritual infancy into maturity. His pastoral walk is grounded in home — he leads the family of Nehemia Worship Center from the overflow of his own family life, where prayer and the Word are daily bread.

He is passionate about raising families rooted in prayer, mentoring vijana in identity and purpose, and cultivating a worshiping church that walks in power and humility.

"Shepherd the flock of God among you, serving as overseers." — 1 Peter 5:2
